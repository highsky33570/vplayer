# Content ingest policy

## Allowed

- Admin upload of media you own or are licensed to distribute
- Partner APIs / feeds under written license
- Using an authorized play URL from a partner **without** copying their library into R2 unless the contract allows it

## Not allowed in this codebase

- Crawlers that scrape https://www.olehdtv.com/ (or similar sites) to download and re-host videos/covers on R2

Re-hosting scraped commercial VOD content is copyright infringement. The platform is built for licensed assets + HLS on R2/TYCDN.
